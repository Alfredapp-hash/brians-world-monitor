The Coverage Compare engine

Every story is clustered across mainstream wires, independent outlets, state-affiliated media, and local press — then compared, phrase by phrase.

Talking-point detection

When multiple outlets use the same distinctive phrasing, that's either wire copy (labeled, benign) or coordinated messaging (flagged in red). The exact repeated phrase is shown with every outlet using it.

Recurring-narrative tracking

One synchronized burst can be coincidence. The same phrase pushed for days across outlets is a campaign — recurring narratives get flagged with their age and full source list.

Blackout detection

Stories independent media covers that mainstream ignores — and stories with wall-to-wall mainstream coverage but zero independent corroboration — are surfaced as coverage asymmetries.

Local press, bridged

Cross-language entity matching pulls Russian, Arabic, Spanish, and other local coverage into the same story cluster, so you see what the press on the ground says — not just what's written about them.

The NCI Engineered Reality score

Every story is scored against a 20-indicator manipulation rubric — suspicious timing, scripted messaging, emotional manipulation, manufactured outrage, false dilemmas, suppression of dissent, and more. Roughly half auto-score from live data; your local AI scores the rest on demand, and you can override any indicator with your own judgment.

0–20 Low
21–40 Some techniques
41–60 Significant
61–80 Organized campaign
81–100 Engineered reality

The scale measures indicators of coordinated manipulation — it does not by itself prove a psyop exists.
